# investor-due-diligence

> Investors run due diligence on you. Now you run due diligence on them.
> 投资人不停问你要数据、要 traction，对你做尽调。但他是谁？见面之前，先看评级。

🌐 https://uniqueclub.ai

## What It Does

A Michelin-style 3-star rating system for evaluating investors before you take a meeting. Input an investor's name, get a data-backed rating and a clear action recommendation in 30 seconds.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Score | Meaning | Action |
|--------|-------|---------|--------|
| ⭐⭐⭐ | ≥ 3.0 | **Worth a Special Trip** (值得专程拜访) | Book a flight to meet them |
| ⭐⭐ | ≥ 2.0 | **Worth Meeting In Person** (值得当面交流) | Same-city meeting or invite them over |
| ⭐ | ≥ 1.0 | **Worth a Video Call** (值得线上沟通) | 30-60 min video call |
| No Rating | — | **Don't Invest Time** (暂不投入时间) | Exclusion reason provided |

## Six Dimensions (1-4 Scale)

- **Dry Powder (25%)** — Do they have money to deploy?
- **Sector Fit (25%)** — Are they investing in your space?
- **Decision Power (20%)** — Can they say yes?
- **Activity (15%)** — How fast do they move?
- **Lead Role (10%)** — Do they lead rounds?
- **Risk (5%)** — Any red flags?

## 13 Override Rules

Calibrated from 120 real investor case studies across Chinese and US markets:

- **6 Downgrade rules**: Sector+Activity double-low, conflict of interest, stage mismatch, power decay, CVC ceiling, angel limitation
- **1 Upgrade rule**: Sector focus bonus (>70% of fund portfolio)
- **4 Exclusion rules**: Unverifiable, departed, cooling period, hard red flag
- **2 Labeling rules**: Same fund different people, FA/banker tagging

## Why This Matters

Founders meet 60-90 investors to close a round. ~70% of those meetings are wasted time. This skill helps you cut the noise and focus on the investors most likely to fund you.
